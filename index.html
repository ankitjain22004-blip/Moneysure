Investments

Mutual funds, SIP & SWP — explained simply

Mutual funds pool money from many investors into a portfolio managed by professionals. They are market-linked: returns are not guaranteed and can be lower than expected. Investing regularly, staying diversified and holding for the long term are principles that may help manage risk.

Equity funds

Invest mainly in company shares; higher potential growth with higher volatility. Better suited to long horizons.

Debt funds

Invest in bonds and similar instruments; generally lower volatility, but not risk-free.

Hybrid funds

Blend equity and debt to balance growth and stability.

Goal-based investing

Link investments to goals — education, home, retirement — with a suitable time horizon.

Portfolio Management Services (PMS)

PMS is a professionally managed, customised portfolio of securities held in an investor's own account, typically for higher-net-worth investors. It involves higher minimum investments, specific fees and higher risk, and is not suitable for everyone. Please understand eligibility, fees and risks before considering PMS. Contact us to discuss whether it is relevant for you; current minimum requirements must be confirmed with the specific provider.

Frequently asked questions

Are mutual fund returns guaranteed?

No. They are market-linked and can fall as well as rise.

Will my loan definitely be approved?

No. Approval and rates depend on the lender's policy and your credit profile and eligibility.

What is the difference between SIP and SWP?

A SIP invests a fixed amount regularly; an SWP withdraws a fixed amount regularly from an existing investment.
